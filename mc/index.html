Features of the Realm

Guild Territories

Stake claim to your own lands. Protect your faction's strongholds, vaults, and towns from roving bandits using intuitive territory claims.

Merchant Economy

Trade wares with local merchants in the spawn tavern, set up custom player shops, and trade rare minerals to amass your guild's treasury.

Undertake seasonal guild quests, slay mythological monstrosities, and reap diamond bounties for your faction.
